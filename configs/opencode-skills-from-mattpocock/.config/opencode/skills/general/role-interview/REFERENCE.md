# Question Bank

Ask these domains in order. For each, start with the **opening question**, then drill deeper with follow-ups based on the user's answers. Skip domains the user says are irrelevant.

## 1. Identity & Context

**Opening**: What's your official title and how long have you been in this role?

- What team or department are you in? What's its mission?
- Who do you report to? Do you have direct reports? How many?
- How would you describe your position to someone outside the company?

## 2. Responsibilities & Scope

**Opening**: What are your three core responsibilities?

- What are you held accountable for — not just "responsible", but actually measured on?
- What decisions can you make autonomously without approval?
- What's explicitly *not* your job, even though people might assume it is?
- What percentage of your time is reactive (requests, incidents) vs proactive (strategy, building)?

## 3. Current Work & Projects

**Opening**: What are you working on right now?

- What's the most impactful thing you've shipped in the last 6 months?
- What's on your roadmap for the next 3-6 months?
- What project or initiative would you champion if you had unlimited resources?
- What's something you've been wanting to do but haven't had time for?

## 4. Technical & Domain Expertise

**Opening**: What does your tech stack look like day-to-day?

- What technologies or systems do you know deeply? Which ones do you just use?
- What domain or business knowledge do you have that others on the team lack?
- What tools, scripts, or workflows do you rely on that aren't obvious?
- If a new hire joined your team, what would they need to learn first?

## 5. Stakeholders & Relationships

**Opening**: Who are the five people you interact with most for work?

- Who depends on your output? What happens if you're blocked?
- Which teams or people do you depend on? Where are the friction points?
- Who advocates for you? Who challenges you?
- How's your relationship with leadership — visibility, trust, alignment?

## 6. Challenges & Growth

**Opening**: What's the hardest part of your role right now?

- What's your biggest blocker or frustration?
- What feedback have you received repeatedly?
- What skill are you actively trying to build?
- Where do you want to be professionally in 1-2 years?
- What would make you leave this role?

## 7. Process & Culture

**Opening**: Walk me through how a typical task or project lands on your plate.

- What meetings or ceremonies matter most? Which ones are a waste?
- What are the unwritten rules someone new would need to know?
- How is performance evaluated? What actually determines raises and promotions?
- How does information flow — are you usually in the loop or out of it?

## 8. Impact & Metrics

**Opening**: How is success measured for your role?

- What numbers or outcomes do you track personally?
- What are you most proud of in this role?
- If you left tomorrow, what would break or slow down?
- What's the one thing you'd change if you became your boss tomorrow?
